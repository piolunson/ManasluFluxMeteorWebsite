Commands

Type them in Meteor's command prompt. Click one to copy it.

HUD elements

Add them from the HUD editor, in the “Manaslu Flux” group.

Image

Shows a png, jpg, bmp or the first frame of a gif, from an absolute path or relative to .minecraft. Scale, opacity and smooth/nearest filtering.

GIF

Animated GIFs with each frame's real delay. Only re-uploads a frame when it changes. Same settings as Image.

Example

The template element: “ManasluFlux” on a gray quad. Handy as a starting point for your own.

Player List

A box with all the other players on the server, sorted by name or ping — color-coded ms, custom title, max rows. Drag and scale it in the HUD editor.

Server Info

Your server's name and address, online player count and your live latency, color-coded. Dark box, rows toggle individually.

Session Info

In-game day number, HH:MM clock, weather (Clear/Rain/Thunder) and the biome you are standing in — the same overworld clock the .mfday command uses.

Kill Counter

Session combat tracker: kills (players and/or hostile mobs, friends excluded), deaths and computed K/D. Auto-reset on world or dimension change.

Changelog

  1. 1.15.14Firework Elytra Fly no longer rotates you at all. 1.15.13 aimed the camera at your movement direction, which put a rotation on the server every tick and made climbing point at the sky. The module now reads your yaw and never writes it, and sends no look packets: direction comes from the keys relative to your view, climb and dive are straight up and down with the sideways drift zeroed, and the whole flight is a velocity controller - jump / sneak, horizontal-speed (20 blocks/second), vertical-speed (8) and the new responsiveness (0.35) - while rockets keep firing every firework-delay (0.8 s) as the thrust. Hover and fake fly are unchanged. Counts unchanged: 69 modules, Movement 6.
  2. 1.15.13Firework Elytra Fly: straight up and down, and no more spinning. Jump with no WASD now aims the rocket straight up and sneak straight down - a full 90° with the sideways velocity zeroed - so vertical flight is a vertical line. A/D no longer spins: the direction was measured from the yaw the module itself wrote every tick, adding another 90° each tick and dragging the camera round; it is now measured from a reference yaw that only follows your mouse and re-bases when the keys change. Counts unchanged: 69 modules, Movement 6.
  3. 1.15.12Firework Elytra Fly is a flight controller now. Jump climbs, sneak descends, W/A/S/D move, and the rocket is the thrust: one is lit in your steering direction whenever you are under the set speed, with horizontal-speed (20 blocks/second) and vertical-speed (8) as the ceiling so the speeds stay stable. With no key held, hover stops the glide and holds your position where you let go, and firework-delay (0.8 s) spaces the rockets out. auto-takeoff now only leaves the ground when you press jump. fake-fly (chestplate, hotbar elytra swapped into the server chest slot for the start-gliding packet) is unchanged. Counts unchanged: 69 modules, Movement 6.
  4. 1.15.11New module: Firework Elytra Fly (firework-elytra-fly, Movement). Elytra flight flown with fireworks: auto-fireworks fires a rocket only while your horizontal speed is under target-speed (35 blocks/second by default) and at most every firework-delay (1.5 s), from a silent hotbar swap put straight back. auto-takeoff, lock-pitch / pitch for level rockets, stop-in-water, and toggle-elytra (elytra on at enable, chestplate back at disable). fake-fly is the chestplate trick from Stashhunting's ElytraFlyPlusPlus: the hotbar elytra is swapped into the server's chest slot for one start-gliding packet and straight back, so you glide while wearing a chestplate and use no elytra durability. Counts: 69 modules, Movement 6.
